Privacy.

Local by design

The driftline CLI performs all parsing, hashing, capture, and reporting on your machine. It has no telemetry, analytics, remote polling, or network code. Snapshot contents, key names, actors, fingerprints, ledgers, and reports are never sent to us.

Website and demo

The website demo uses fixed fictional data. It does not accept or upload configuration files. The site uses no third-party fonts, scripts, ad technology, or behavioral analytics. The service worker stores public site files in your browser for offline use.

Licenses

If you buy or restore Pro, checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo. Their checkout receives the purchase information you submit. This site stores the returned license token and its last verification verdict in your browser's local storage. Verification sends only that token to api.sociobot.in; it never sends configuration data. You can remove it by clearing this site's storage.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions about purchase records, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
